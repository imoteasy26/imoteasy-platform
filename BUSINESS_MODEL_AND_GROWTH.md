# Business Model and Growth

## Broker acquisition offer

Every newly activated broker receives 30 days of access to the configurable professional (medium) tier, including broker CRM, listing and lead management, a marketing workspace, performance reporting, and the future individualized AI marketing department.

The trial begins only when the broker completes the required onboarding checklist and explicitly activates the workspace. It requires no payment card. It does not renew into a paid plan and never triggers an automatic charge. Expiry moves access to a clearly disclosed expired/read-only state unless the broker explicitly chooses an available next step. No subscription prices are specified in this model.

Plan names, feature entitlements, limits, and trial duration are stored as configurable data. Usage events are measured per broker and organization. A later billing launch requires approved prices, terms, tax/accounting treatment, consent, cancellation, and a separate explicit authorization flow.

## Network growth loops

1. **Marketplace supply:** seller and broker listings create useful public inventory.
2. **Broker activation:** qualified inquiries, assignments, follow-ups, and viewings make the broker workspace valuable beyond listing publication.
3. **Outcome learning:** verified lead, viewing, offer, and transaction events improve measured performance and workflow quality.
4. **Investor demand:** opt-in criteria and transparent alerts attract repeat buyers without implying guaranteed returns.
5. **Agency expansion:** team assignment and visibility make successful brokers a distribution path for agency adoption.

Growth is measured from consented, first-party events. No platform metric should be fabricated when a channel does not expose it.

## Private seller incentive

A seller may be offered a 1% bonus funded from a broker's agreed commission only under an approved written agreement. The exact basis remains unresolved and must be configurable among:

- percentage of transaction price;
- percentage points deducted from the broker's agreed commission rate; or
- percentage share of the broker's actual fee.

No model is selected by default. No reward is promised, calculated as payable, or paid until legal terms, commission basis, accounting treatment, verification, approval, and dispute process are approved. The signed agreement and recorded actual broker fee are the source of truth.

## Investor value proposition

Investors can define budgets, locations, property types, price/area ranges, expected rent, gross/net yield goals, renovation constraints, strategy, and required discounts. Alerts cover new opportunities, price reductions, and potential underpricing, with data coverage and confidence visible. “Potentially underpriced” means an explainable signal from available comparables, not a representation of true market value.

Asking prices, verified completed transaction prices, broker estimates, and investor-entered assumptions are displayed and stored separately.

## Subscription and usage readiness

The first release prepares product entitlements and usage ledgers but does not charge a payment method. No final price, billing processor, recurring renewal, or AI quota is hardcoded. Configurable limits must be enforceable server-side and visible to the broker before an action is blocked.

## Attribution and measurement

Record broker referral, campaign/channel, UTM context, listing, broker, first source, last source, inquiry, viewing, offer, and verified completion. Use a documented attribution model and date range. Do not use invasive fingerprinting, scrape audience data, or infer a conversion without an attributable event.

Marketing reports distinguish measured metrics from unavailable ones. Revenue is shown only when verifiably recorded; marketing spend and AI costs are tied to their source and period.

## Trust and legal gates

- No automatic subscription charge after trial.
- No reward payout before approved agreements and verified transaction completion.
- No claim of below-market value without sufficient comparable transaction evidence.
- No unsolicited personal messaging or unsupported social publishing.
- No use of personal information for optional marketing without the required lawful basis and opt-out controls.
